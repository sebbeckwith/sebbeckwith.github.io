# 1. COMPANY / PRODUCT CONTEXT

Gobby is a qualitative-first survey and insight platform.

It is designed for organisations that need to understand what people actually think, feel, experience, need and prioritise — rather than only count answers to predefined multiple-choice questions.

Gobby is not intended to be positioned as another generic form builder.

Its central premise is that conventional survey tools are often built around closed questions, scales and predefined answers. Those methods are useful in some situations, but they can miss context, nuance, unexpected issues and lived experience.

Gobby is designed around richer qualitative feedback.

A useful working definition is:

> Gobby helps organisations collect open-text feedback at scale and turn messy human responses into clear themes, shared insight and decisions.

Another useful framing is:

> Qualitative-first survey software for organisations that need to understand what people really think — not just count checkbox answers.

The product is new to market and currently needs:
- credibility
- clearer positioning
- stronger conversion
- stronger differentiation
- better visual identity
- better SEO foundations
- better explanation of how the product works
- early customer proof
- a website that looks meaningfully different from generic SaaS

Do NOT over-position Gobby as an AI product.

AI is a supporting capability, not the core story.

# 2. CORE PRODUCT CAPABILITIES

The homepage should communicate the following product ideas clearly.

## Open-text qualitative feedback

Gobby allows people to respond in their own words.

It is especially useful when the organisation asking the question does not already know all the possible answers.

## Peer matching / shared responses

Participants may be shown anonymised responses from other people that are relevant to what they have written.

This helps identify shared experiences, resonance and collective priorities.

This should NOT be framed simplistically as a popularity contest.

Think:
- shared meaning
- relevant experiences
- peer validation
- collective insight

## Peer voting / agreement

Participants can agree with or validate relevant responses.

This helps surface what resonates across a group.

## AI-assisted qualitative analysis

Gobby can assist with:
- identifying possible themes
- grouping responses
- thematic analysis
- making large volumes of open-text feedback manageable

However:

AI assistance should always be presented as supporting human judgement.

Avoid language suggesting:
- fully autonomous research
- magic AI understanding
- replacing researchers
- guaranteed accuracy
- one-click truth

Good principle:

> AI-assisted. Human-led.

## Reporting / action

The value does not stop at data collection.

The broader journey is:

Listen → Make Sense → Act → Measure Impact

The website should communicate that Gobby helps move from responses to useful insight and action.

# 3. WHY GOBBY EXISTS

Gobby challenges a familiar pattern:

Organisations ask people for feedback.

People complete long surveys.

The survey contains predefined scales and tick boxes.

Open comments get buried in spreadsheets.

Reports are produced.

Participants often never see what happened next.

Gobby should challenge this model without pretending that all quantitative research is bad.

The enemy is NOT statistics.

The enemy is shallow, performative or badly designed listening.

A useful internal brand belief is:

> Understanding people starts by giving them room to answer in their own words.

Another:

> More answers. Fewer assumptions.

Another:

> The organisation asking the question should not always decide the possible answers in advance.

The brand should have a point of view.
